## Recommendation in five sentences

Keep classifier_v1 authoritative and withhold activation of gemini-3.8-flash: it passes 5 of 10 release gates as a full candidate and 4 of 6 as the proposed cohort `MEASURED`. Its macro F1 is 0.885 against a floor of 0.893 `MEASURED`, and 4 sensitive intents miss the 0.95 recall gate, the lowest at 0.60 `MEASURED`. Where the two models agree, auto-routed precision rises to 0.976 `MEASURED`, and the LLM routes 90% of out-of-domain risk cases to abstain against 0% for the classifier `MEASURED`. In shadow, the models agreed on 85.5% of labels over 1,540 cases `SIMULATED`, at 1.65 USD of model spend `MEASURED`, and the proposed cohort would have produced +151 USD of net value over the 14 days compared with the classifier alone `ASSUMPTION`. The next investment is targeted: fix the sensitive-intent confusions (card payment versus direct debit) and re-evaluate, rather than spending on a larger model.

## What did not match the intended demo story

The recorded decisions follow the measured numbers. No activation was recorded, so monitoring and the incident drill run on classifier_v1, which stays in production (docs/incident_report.md).
